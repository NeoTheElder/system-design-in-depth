Real-time Updates

A JEE-style paper on getting changes from servers to clients, and from wherever they happen to the right server. Part A tests the pattern itself; Part B tests it inside eleven interview problems, from Ticketmaster to ChatGPT. Part C goes deeper into routing, coordination and failure, and carries the ideas into other systems.
